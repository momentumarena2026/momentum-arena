# Session 2026-08-16 → 2026-08-24

Everything below reached `main`. Ordered as it happened, because several
items only make sense as consequences of the one before.

## Cricket scoring

**Crease is server-owned.** Strike rotates on odd runs and swaps at the
over from the folded event log, rather than waiting to be re-tagged by
hand. The over-end bowler prompt opens itself. Overs are captured at the
toss and stored per match.

**Wickets-per-innings became a tournament setting.** It was hardcoded at
ten while Momentum's cup plays eight, which silently disabled the Net Run
Rate rule that charges a bowled-out side its full over quota — three wrong
NRRs in a live pool. New cricket tournaments default to 10 overs / 8
wickets rather than to a blank that turns the tiebreaker off.

**A cricket match now refuses to start without 1–90 overs.** Zero reads as
"unlimited" downstream and switches off both the innings close and that
same NRR charge. Refusing at the toss is the only cheap moment.

**Dismissals became real.** `lib/cricket-dismissal.ts` is the single
authority on whether a wicket is the bowler's and how the scorecard line
reads. A run-out is not the bowler's wicket — crediting it inflated both
his analysis and the Most Wickets trophy. The pad asks who caught or ran
them out, and lines degrade to the shortest TRUE statement (`caught`, not
`c — b —`) because matches scored before fielder capture have no fielder
and never will.

**Run out was unreachable.** It sat below five kind-rows in a sheet with
no `maxHeight`, so its ScrollView had no bounded parent: the rows existed,
were laid out past the screen edge, and no gesture could reach them.
Reproduced on an emulator. Run out is now a sixth peer row.

**Extras.** The pad could only say wide, no-ball or bye, each hardcoded to
one run; leg byes were never emitted although the engine always accepted
them. Adding the real combinations exposed two live errors: byes and leg
byes were charged to the bowler (they are the keeper's), and a no-ball hit
for four credited the batter nothing. `batterRunsOf` / `bowlerRunsOf` now
decide, shared by the fold and the scorecard.

## Tournament brackets

**A bye no longer creates a match nobody plays.** With three qualifiers the
generator built a real "Team vs BYE" fixture and pointed the Final at its
winner; deleting that unplayable match — the obvious thing to do — left the
Final permanently unresolvable, which is exactly what happened to the live
cup. Byes now collapse at build time. Plus a guard against deleting a
fixture another round feeds from, and `assignMatchTeam` as the manual
escape hatch for a bracket already stuck.

## Money and reporting

**Three surfaces reported "sports earnings" and all three differed** —
bookings-only vs every stream, booked vs received. They answer different
questions and now say so on screen; the bookings tile is renamed *Booking
Earnings*. `scripts/reconcile-sports-earnings.ts` decomposes the gap.

**The one real bug it found:** every cash figure filters on
`Payment.confirmedAt`, so six COMPLETED payments with a null timestamp were
money in the till no cash report could see. Backfilled, then fixed at
source — three write paths (`markRemainderCollected`, `editPayment`,
`closeOutBooking`) flipped a payment to COMPLETED without stamping it.
Closeout dates to the SESSION, not the button press, so bulk-closing last
month does not book its cash into this one.

**Partial venue collection.** The server always allowed it; the app hard
-blocked anything but the exact full amount, and the app is what the front
desk uses at the counter.

## Platform attribution

`createBookingFromHold` defaulted `platform` to `"web"` and **nine of ten
call sites omitted it** — and those nine cannot pass it, because a PhonePe
callback and a Razorpay webhook arrive from their servers, never from the
customer's device. Every DQR, app-Razorpay, pass and webhook booking was
recorded as web. The platform is now stamped on the `SlotHold` (the last
point a device is talking to us) and the argument falls back to it.

All 588 existing bookings said web; only 13 were recoverable from the audit
trail, none older than 2026-08-06 because `ServerActionLog` has retention.
**Read the other 575 as unknown, not as web.**

## UPI journey instrumentation

`upi_app_selected` finally names which app the customer chose, recorded
*before* the handoff so an app that fails to open is still attributable —
otherwise the apps that break are the ones missing from the data. Around it
sit checkout_opened, qr_shown, confirmed, expired, abandoned, claimed_paid,
each with surface, app and seconds-since-QR. Web and app share names
exactly.

Server side: the DQR status route separates *settled-by-callback* from
*settled-by-poll* — a rising poll share is the early warning that S2S
callbacks are failing. The static-QR callback logged nothing at all despite
always answering 200, hiding failed checksums, missing UTRs and amount
mismatches.

## Infrastructure

**Neon compute charges from branch creation.** Any pushed branch made
Vercel create a deployment and the Neon integration provisioned a database
branch for it. `vercel-ignore.sh` only skipped the *build* —
`ignoreCommand` runs inside the deployment pipeline, too late. Fixed with
`git.deploymentEnabled` in `vercel.json` using `**` (not `*`, which does
not cross a slash, so `claude/*` would have kept deploying). Neon's own
GitHub integration, if enabled, is separate and must be turned off there.
